We automate the real apps on real Android phones: phone farms, WhatsApp, Instagram, and any app that has no API. The MationX Client app runs on your phones, your own or in the cloud; we build the flows, run them and keep them working after every app update.

### What we do

- **[Phone farm automation](https://mationx.com)**: account warm-up, daily activity and sending from your backend, across your fleet
- **[WhatsApp API on real phones](https://mationx.com/whatsapp-api)**: your numbers in the real WhatsApp app, behind one REST endpoint, with an AI agent
- **[Instagram DM automation](https://mationx.com/instagram-dm-automation)**: auto replies, comment-to-DM and follow-ups in the real Instagram app
- **[Android MCP](https://mationx.com/android-mcp)**: give Claude, ChatGPT or Cursor a real Android phone and its apps

### Open source

- **[whatsapp-mcp](https://github.com/petrkyncl/whatsapp-mcp)**: WhatsApp in Claude, ChatGPT or Cursor, with the full history from your phone
